What their pages ask readers to do

    
Share of each company's pages (same period) that include each call to action. A page can have more than one. "Pages" counts only pages the collector has opened and checked. Notes appear only with 5+ checked pages.

    
Checked by hand: pages under No call to action are regular blog posts. They inform the reader but don't ask them to start a trial, book a demo or download anything inside the article.
